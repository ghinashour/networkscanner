Prerequisite: Turn on 2-Step Verification
Google requires 2-Step Verification to be enabled before you can create an app password.

Go to your Google Account: myaccount.google.com

Click Security in the left sidebar.

Under “How you sign in to Google”, click 2-Step Verification and turn it ON.

Follow the prompts to verify your account with your phone.

Step-by-Step: Generate the App Password
Once 2-Step Verification is active:

Go directly to the App Passwords page:
myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
(You may need to sign in again.)

Enter a name for the app or device (e.g., FlaskApp, NetworkScanner, or any name you’ll recognise).

Click Create – Google will generate a 16‑digit password.

Copy the password immediately and paste it into your .env file.
⚠️ Important: Google shows this code only once – if you lose it, you’ll need to generate a new one.
